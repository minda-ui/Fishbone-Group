## The change request

- **Ref:** FC-CR-0001 · **Raised by:** Fishbone Construction · **Type:** clarification · **Against:** policy v1.1
- **Substance (two points):**
  1. Are recurring **tasks / to-dos** (e.g. an automation's action items) "documents" that need a register row and an ID?
  2. When may a KB migrate its **existing local back-catalogue** (Properties `FP…`, Holdings `FH…`, etc.) into the group register — now, or only after a central migration?

## Owner decisions (2026-09-09)

- **Q1 — tasks are not documents.** A task / to-do is not a business record and gets **no** register row and **no** document ID. Only documents meaningful to the record or audit trail are registered (policy §6). Decision recorded: *"No — tasks aren't documents."*
- **Q2 — each KB migrates its own back-catalogue now.** Rather than waiting for a single central migration, **each KB may migrate its own local back-catalogue into the group register at any time**, deduping on entry (Source key + title/date/counterparty) so nothing is double-numbered. Decision recorded: *"Each KB migrates its own now."*
