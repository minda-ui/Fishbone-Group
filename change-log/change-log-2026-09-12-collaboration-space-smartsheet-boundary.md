## What this session did

Added a new §10, "Collaboration Space & Smartsheet boundary," to `Process-Fishbone-Systems-House-Rules.md`
(the shared house rules document, `Wiki/` folder of this database), bumping it from v1.1 to **v1.2**.
The new section carries the two rules from the Stage 2 proposal's Part B essentially verbatim:

- **Collaboration Space** is the source of operational documents (invoices, correspondence, day-to-day
  paperwork, per company); a KB cites specific documents from it but never re-files, copies, or
  duplicates the whole folder into its own `Raw/` or `Outputs/`.
- **Smartsheet** holds live working data (property registers, order trackers, loan schedules, task
  lists); a KB never copies a Smartsheet figure into prose without a citation to the specific sheet
  and a "read live, don't trust a stale figure" reminder next to it.

Both rules apply group-wide (every company KB and the Group database) the same way §8 (propagate) and
§9 (Sandbox boundary) do — no company `CLAUDE.md` needs to be rewritten for this to take effect.
