## The plan (owner-stated intention, not yet executed)

- **Move** the group's **woodworking machinery** — currently on **Fishbone Construction Ltd's** balance sheet (plant and machinery cost £248,200, part under a Haydock Finance hire-purchase agreement) — onto **Fishbone Holdings Ltd's** balance sheet.
- **Then lease** the machinery **from Holdings to Amfa Furniture Ltd** under an intra-group lease.
- **Purpose: asset protection** — ring-fencing the machinery in the non-trading parent, insulated from the trading companies' operational and creditor risk, while the furniture trade uses it under lease.
- This would be a material change to Holdings, presently a pure holding company with no trade: it would acquire a plant asset and earn lease income.

Open sub-questions for **RMT** to structure (recorded, not actioned here): the transfer value; the outstanding Haydock Finance HP over the machines (moving HP assets needs the financier's involvement); the capital-allowances and VAT position; and the intra-group lease terms. This sits alongside OI-13's other open items (which entity invoices the AMFA order-tracker customers; the 50/50 leasehold-improvement apportionment).
