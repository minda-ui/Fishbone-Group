## Summary
The legal parent of three group companies (Fishbone Construction Ltd, Fishbone Waste Ltd and Furniture by Fishbone Ltd) and a lender to a fourth (Fishbone Properties Ltd), which it does not own but which is under common control. A pure holding company with no trade, two directors, two £1 shares, and net assets of £436,959 at 30 April 2025. Its only income is interest on the Properties Ltd loans and, in FY2025, a £128,000 dividend from Construction. Minda sees it as the natural home for group-level strategy, governance and capital allocation. It now has its own dedicated Knowledge Base (created 2026-09-05); interest on the Properties Ltd loans was **waived by executed agreement on 2026-09-07** for 1 Oct 2026–30 Sep 2028. A group restructuring is **planned (not yet executed)** under which the group's woodworking machinery would move onto Holdings' balance sheet and be leased to Amfa Furniture Ltd for **asset protection** — which would give Holdings its first plant asset and lease income, and its first trading-style activity (see the planned-restructure note below and `open-issues.md` OI-13).

### Relationship to the rest of the group
The group is a legal group of four (Holdings plus three subsidiaries) beside two sister companies under common control (Properties Ltd and Commercial Properties Ltd) and the SSAS. Holdings' balance sheet is almost entirely the Properties Ltd loans and small subsidiary balances. Its FY2025 profit came from the £128,000 dividend Construction paid up in a year when Construction itself made a loss, which is worth noting for any group cash-flow view.

### Planned: machinery holding and lease to Amfa (OI-13, not yet executed)
Owner intention recorded 2026-09-08 (resolving OI-8): the group's **woodworking machinery** — currently on Fishbone Construction Ltd's balance sheet (plant and machinery cost £248,200, part under a Haydock Finance hire-purchase agreement) — would be **moved onto Fishbone Holdings Ltd's balance sheet** and then **leased from Holdings to Amfa Furniture Ltd**. The stated purpose is **asset protection**: holding the machinery in the non-trading parent ring-fences it from the trading companies' operational and creditor risk, while the furniture trade uses it under an intra-group lease. This would be a material change to Holdings, which is presently a pure holding company with no trade — it would acquire a plant asset and earn lease income. It is currently in **research mode** — being explored internally on the Minda side; **RMT is not to be involved until there is a plan**, which will then need checking (the structuring questions — transfer value, the outstanding Haydock HP, capital-allowances and VAT position, and the lease terms — are for that later checking stage, not now). This database records the intention only and takes no accounting or filing action. See `open-issues.md` OI-13, `Org-Fishbone-Construction-Ltd.md` and `Org-Amfa-Furniture-Ltd.md`. [S15]
