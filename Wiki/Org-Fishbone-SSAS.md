## Summary
The group's occupational pension scheme — a Small Self-Administered Scheme (SSAS) — used as an internal lender to group companies. It is a trust, not a company. Its two members are the directors, Mindaugas Gaudiesius and Andrejus Prutkovas. Its one recorded investment to date is a secured loanback to Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd (£41,500), charged over that company's freehold at 145 High Street East. It now has its own dedicated Knowledge Base (created 2026-09-06), which is the canonical home for scheme records; this article is the group-level index entry that links to it.

### Relationship to the rest of the group
The SSAS is the group's internal pension vehicle and lender. Its members and two of its trustees are the group's directors; its principal employer is Fishbone Construction Ltd. Its only recorded external-facing position is the secured loanback to Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd, which is one of the three "debt layers" recorded in the group loan reconciliation (see `open-issues.md` OI-6 and the Loan Repayment Plan workbook). Because it is a registered pension scheme, anything touching it (contributions, investments, loanbacks, member benefits) is subject to pensions law and its own trustees/administrator — outside this database's remit; this article records only what the group needs to know it exists and how it connects.
