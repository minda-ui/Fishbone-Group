## Summary
A waste removal and disposal business, wholly owned by Fishbone Holdings Ltd. It has never made a profit: losses of £50,078 in FY2023, £46,054 in FY2024 and £90,435 in FY2025 left it with net liabilities of £197,152 at 30 April 2025, funded almost entirely by loans from Fishbone Construction Ltd and Fishbone Holdings Ltd. Despite that, the FY2025 accounts (approved 28 April 2026) show it **still trading** on a going-concern basis, with six employees, turnover of £111,882 and £51,384 of new assets bought in the year, including a £22,499 vehicle and £25,000 of leasehold improvements. The Minda Wiki of 18 Aug 2026 describes it as "inactive", so it may have wound down between April 2025 and August 2026; that is not yet confirmed. Minda regards it as an important institutional lesson about numbers, accountability and operating discipline, to be preserved rather than treated only as a failed company.

### Reading the numbers
The business roughly doubled turnover in its second full year (FY2024) but administrative costs doubled too, mostly wages and vehicle running. FY2025 turnover held near £112,000 while admin costs rose again to £175,749, deepening the loss. It was financed by Construction and Holdings lending it money rather than by external debt; group undertakings owed rose to £212,709. Construction's FY2025 accounts still carry the £128,109 as a debtor, so if Waste is wound up without repaying, that is a write-off in Construction's books.

## Open questions
- Is the company dormant at Companies House, struck off, or still trading in a reduced form? The FY2025 accounts (to April 2025) show active trading; the Minda Wiki (Aug 2026) says "inactive". Confirm the current position at Companies House.
- How the £128,109 owed to Construction and £84,700 owed to Holdings will be treated if the company is wound up.
- The lesson Minda wants preserved has not been written down anywhere surveyed. A retrospective article is needed.
- Whether any liabilities (leases, vehicle finance, waste carrier licence obligations) remain open.
