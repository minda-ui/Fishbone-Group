## Summary
The group's residential property portfolio company. It holds and lets a portfolio of houses in the North East, each tracked under an FP reference code, and is the best-documented entity on Drive with its own actively maintained knowledge base. Two properties are earmarked for sale to repay group debt. This is a stub created on 2026-09-03 from existing Drive material.

## Key facts
- Distinct legal entity from Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd despite the similar name; confirmed 21 Aug 2026. [S1][S2]
- Portfolio of 17 properties on Drive, referenced FP1601 to FP2401 (year of acquisition plus sequence): 37 Firtrees Avenue, 77 Martin Road, 51 Wheatfield Grove, 3 Byeways, 35 Coronation Street, 49 Wheatfield Grove, 28 Ongar Way, 28 North Terrace, 12 Garth Twentytwo, 38 Glebe Road, 2 Croft Avenue, 17 St Juliens Gardens, 99 Melrose Gardens, 2 Ferndale Avenue, 3 Basingstoke Place, 131 Goathland Avenue. [S3][S4]
- Strategic target: add roughly four to six properties per year. Owner pay target referenced at £4k to £5k per month; £0 reported at the 18 Aug 2026 check-in. [S5]
- Two facilities in the Loans Wiki: a £35,000 related-party loan (Sebastian, interest-only, in arrears, no written agreement) and Funding Circle 0874BD (£39,613 balance, £1,272 per month as at 21 Aug 2026). [S1]
- Planned sale of 49 Wheatfield Grove (FP2001) and 3 The Byeways (FP1901), combined net equity roughly £143,677 before costs and tax, proceeds to be applied to group debt. Nothing agreed as at 25 Aug 2026. Selling would remove £1,845 per month of rent. [S1][S2]
- Also a drawer on the Funding Circle Book 2 revolving trade-finance facility. [S1]
- The Fishbone Properties team also manages Fishbone Commercial Properties. [S5]
