# Amfa Furniture Ltd (registered as Furniture by Fishbone Ltd, formerly Fishbone Investment Ltd)

**Type:** Organisation
**Status:** Active
**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-03
**Related:** [Fishbone Holdings Ltd](Org-Fishbone-Holdings-Ltd.md) · [Fishbone Construction Ltd](Org-Fishbone-Construction-Ltd.md)

## Summary
The group's furniture and panel manufacturing business, positioned as premium bespoke kitchens and wardrobes with a luxury British feel. It is a wholly owned subsidiary of Fishbone Holdings Ltd. Its registered name in the parent's FY2024 accounts (approved April 2025) is **Furniture by Fishbone Ltd**, company 11259604, formerly Fishbone Investment Ltd. Minda refers to it as Amfa Furniture Ltd and its Smartsheet workspace is named "AMFA Furniture"; whether the company has since been renamed at Companies House is not confirmed. Longer-term intention is to systemise the business fully and possibly sell it.

## Key facts
- Registered name Furniture by Fishbone Ltd, formerly Fishbone Investment Limited, company number 11259604, registered at 6 Beverley Place, Wallsend NE28 7BH, per the parent's accounts for the year to 30 April 2024. [S1 note 3]
- Wholly owned subsidiary of Fishbone Holdings Ltd. [S1 note 3][S1 note 7]
- Named by Minda on 2026-09-03 as "Amfa Furniture Ltd", a member of the Fishbone Group. [S2]
- Fishbone Construction Ltd's FY2025 accounts (dated April 2026) still call it "Fishbone Investments Ltd, a fellow subsidiary", owing Construction £100. [S3 note 9]
- Owed Holdings £3,745 at 30 April 2024, interest-free, no set terms. [S1 note 7]
- Described in the Minda Wiki as "Furniture by Fishbone Ltd", premium furniture and panel manufacturing, processes being formalised. [S4]
- Brand naming exercise explored Ampagio, Oreon, Amfa, Amvra, Amvero and others; explicit preference for no "oak" in the name; trademark checking discussed. [S4]
- Owner pay target referenced at £7k+ per month; £0 reported at the 18 Aug 2026 check-in. [S4]
- Longer-term intention: possible sale of the business once fully systemised. [S4]
- Has its own Smartsheet workspace "AMFA Furniture" with an order tracker (statuses Received to Shipped, one Drive folder per order), first order AM001 dated 17 Jul 2026. [S5]
- The woodworking machinery under the Haydock Finance hire-purchase agreement, and £449,536 of leasehold improvements added in FY2025, sit in Fishbone Construction Ltd's accounts, not this company's. [S3 note 4][S6]

### On the name
Three names are in use: Fishbone Investment(s) Ltd (the original registered name, still used in Construction's April 2026 accounts), Furniture by Fishbone Ltd (the registered name per the April 2025 Holdings accounts and the Drive folder), and Amfa / AMFA Furniture (Minda's name and the Smartsheet workspace). The article keeps the filename Minda chose and records the registered name in the title. If Companies House confirms a rename to Amfa Furniture Ltd, only the header text changes; if not, the article should be retitled to Furniture by Fishbone Ltd with a redirect, per `WIKI_GUIDELINES.md` §7.
