## Summary
The group's residential property portfolio company. It holds and lets houses and flats in the North East, each tracked under an FP reference code, and is the best-documented entity on Drive and Smartsheet with its own actively maintained knowledge base. It is **not** a subsidiary of Fishbone Holdings Ltd; the accounts describe the two as under common control. In the year to 30 April 2025 it made a loss of £58,555, its first loss since a smaller £25,469 loss in FY2020, after the investment-property fair-value gain that had carried FY2024 fell to nil and loan interest rose to £76,472. It owed Holdings £303,702 on two interest-bearing loans at 30 April 2025 and owes the wider group besides. Two properties are earmarked for sale to repay group debt.
