## Summary
The group's residential property portfolio company. It holds and lets houses and flats in the North East, each tracked under an FP reference code, and is the best-documented entity on Drive and Smartsheet with its own actively maintained knowledge base. It is **not** a subsidiary of Fishbone Holdings Ltd; the accounts describe the two as under common control. It owes Holdings about £303,000 on two interest-bearing loans and owed Construction £288,807 at 30 April 2024. Two properties are earmarked for sale to repay group debt.

## Key facts
- Company number 09687012, incorporated in England and Wales, private company limited by shares. Registered office 6 Beverley Place, Wallsend NE28 7BH. [S1]
- Directors: A Prutkovas and M Gaudiesius. Three employees including directors. Accountants RMT; bankers HSBC. Accounting reference date 30 April. [S1]
- Principal activity: property rental. Investment properties are held at fair value, assessed by the director on an open market basis. [S1 directors' report; note 5]
- Under common control with Fishbone Holdings Ltd, Fishbone Construction Ltd and Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd; not a subsidiary of Holdings. [S1 note 10][S2 note 8]
- Distinct legal entity from Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd despite the similar name; confirmed 21 Aug 2026. [S3][S4]
- Portfolio of 17 properties on Drive and Smartsheet, referenced FP1601 to FP2401. As at 1 Sep 2026 the register shows rent of £12,784 per month, loans of £1,489,192, value of £2,328,000 and equity of £838,808. [S5][S6]
- Strategic target: add roughly four to six properties per year. Owner pay target referenced at £4k to £5k per month; £0 reported at the 18 Aug 2026 check-in. [S7]
- Two facilities in the Loans Wiki: a £35,000 related-party loan (Sebastian, interest-only, in arrears, no written agreement) and Funding Circle 0874BD (£39,613 balance, £1,272 per month as at 21 Aug 2026). [S3]
- Planned sale of 49 Wheatfield Grove (FP2001) and 3 The Byeways (FP1901), combined net equity roughly £143,677 before costs and tax, proceeds to be applied to group debt. Nothing agreed as at 25 Aug 2026. [S3][S4]
- The Fishbone Properties team also manages Fishbone Commercial Properties. [S7]
