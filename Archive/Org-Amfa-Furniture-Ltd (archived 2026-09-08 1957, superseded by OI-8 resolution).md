# Amfa Furniture Ltd (company 11259604; formerly Furniture by Fishbone Ltd, and before that Fishbone Investment Limited)

**Type:** Organisation
**Status:** Active
**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-06
**Related:** [Fishbone Holdings Ltd](Org-Fishbone-Holdings-Ltd.md) · [Fishbone Construction Ltd](Org-Fishbone-Construction-Ltd.md)

## Summary
The group's furniture and panel manufacturing business, positioned as premium bespoke kitchens and wardrobes with a luxury British feel. It is a wholly owned subsidiary of Fishbone Holdings Ltd. Its current registered name is **Amfa Furniture Ltd**, company 11259604: the company was renamed from Furniture by Fishbone Ltd by special resolution on 13 July 2026, and before that was Fishbone Investment Limited. [S9] This confirms Minda's name for it and settles OI-1. Its Smartsheet workspace is named "AMFA Furniture". Longer-term intention is to systemise the business fully and possibly sell it.

## Key facts
- Current registered name Amfa Furniture Ltd, company number 11259604, registered at 6 Beverley Place, Wallsend NE28 7BH. [S9][S1 note 3]
- Renamed from Furniture by Fishbone Ltd to Amfa Furniture Ltd by special resolution passed on 13 July 2026; Companies House certificate of incorporation on change of name issued the same day. Before Furniture by Fishbone Ltd it was Fishbone Investment Limited. [S9]
- The earlier change of name from **Fishbone Investment Ltd to Furniture by Fishbone Ltd took effect on 10 April 2024** (Companies House certificate of incorporation on change of name, company 11259604). So the name sequence is: Fishbone Investment Ltd → Furniture by Fishbone Ltd (10 Apr 2024) → Amfa Furniture Ltd (13 Jul 2026). [S12]
- Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) **8681623295**, for company 11259604. [S10]
- As **Fishbone Investment Limited** the company was a non-trading shell. Its corporation tax computation and CT600 for the period 1 April 2020 to 31 March 2021 show no turnover, only £69 of bank charges, a £69 pre-tax loss, nil corporation tax, and a few hundred pounds of trading losses carried forward (£445 unused at 31 March 2021). It then had a **31 March** accounting reference date, since aligned to the group's April year-end. The return was signed by director **Mindaugas Gaudiesius** on 16 July 2021. [S10]
- **Company history as Fishbone Investment Ltd (2018 to 2021).** Incorporated **16 March 2018**, originally registered in **London** (44 Broadway, Stratford E15) with sole director Mindaugas Gaudiesius and accountants TaxAce Ltd. Its first accounts (period to 31 March 2019, filed in the misspelled name "Fishbone Investement Ltd") show a non-trading company: nil turnover, a £103 loss, £1,797 cash and net liabilities of £3. By the accounts to 31 March 2021 the registered office had moved to **6 Beverley Place, Newcastle**, **Andrejus Prutkovas** had joined as a second director, and it held £5,687 of assets against £5,587 net assets — still non-trading (nil turnover). It only became the furniture business later, under the names Furniture by Fishbone Ltd and then Amfa Furniture Ltd. [S11][S10]
- Wholly owned subsidiary of Fishbone Holdings Ltd. [S1 note 3][S1 note 7]
- Named by Minda on 2026-09-03 as "Amfa Furniture Ltd", a member of the Fishbone Group; now confirmed as the registered name. [S2][S9]
- Fishbone Construction Ltd's FY2025 accounts (dated April 2026) still call it "Fishbone Investments Ltd, a fellow subsidiary", owing Construction £100. [S3 note 9]
- Owed Holdings £3,745 at 30 April 2024, interest-free, no set terms. [S1 note 7]
- Described in the Minda Wiki as "Furniture by Fishbone Ltd", premium furniture and panel manufacturing, processes being formalised. [S4]
- Brand naming exercise explored Ampagio, Oreon, Amfa, Amvra, Amvero and others; explicit preference for no "oak" in the name; trademark checking discussed. [S4]
- Owner pay target referenced at £7k+ per month; £0 reported at the 18 Aug 2026 check-in. [S4]
- Longer-term intention: possible sale of the business once fully systemised. [S4]
- Has its own Smartsheet workspace "AMFA Furniture" with an order tracker (statuses Received to Shipped, one Drive folder per order), first order AM001 dated 17 Jul 2026. [S5]
- The woodworking machinery under the Haydock Finance hire-purchase agreement, and £449,536 of leasehold improvements added in FY2025, sit in Fishbone Construction Ltd's accounts, not this company's. [S3 note 4][S6]

### On the name
The company has carried three registered names in sequence: **Fishbone Investment Limited** (original), **Furniture by Fishbone Ltd** (the name in the April 2025 Holdings accounts and the Drive folder), and now **Amfa Furniture Ltd** (renamed by special resolution on 13 July 2026). [S9] The article title and filename both use the current registered name, so no redirect is needed. Two lagging references remain: Fishbone Construction Ltd's April 2026 accounts still call it "Fishbone Investments Ltd" [S3 note 9], and the Drive operational folder is still "Furniture by Fishbone"; both pre-date or have not caught up with the rename and are not errors.
