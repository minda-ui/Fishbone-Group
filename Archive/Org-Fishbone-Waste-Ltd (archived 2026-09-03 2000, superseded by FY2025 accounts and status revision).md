# Fishbone Waste Ltd

**Type:** Organisation
**Status:** Dormant
**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-03
**Related:** [Fishbone Holdings Ltd](Org-Fishbone-Holdings-Ltd.md) · [Fishbone Construction Ltd](Org-Fishbone-Construction-Ltd.md)

## Summary
A waste removal and disposal business, wholly owned by Fishbone Holdings Ltd, that traded from about 2022 to 2025 and is now described as inactive. It never made a profit: losses of £50,078 in FY2023 and £46,054 in FY2024 left it with net liabilities of £106,717 at 30 April 2024, funded almost entirely by Fishbone Construction Ltd. Minda regards it as an important institutional lesson about numbers, accountability and operating discipline, to be preserved rather than treated only as a failed company.

### Reading the numbers
The business roughly doubled turnover in its second full year but administrative costs doubled too, mostly wages and vehicle running. It was financed by Construction lending it money rather than by external debt. Construction's FY2025 accounts still carry the £128,109 as a debtor, so if Waste is wound up without repaying, that is a write-off in Construction's books.
