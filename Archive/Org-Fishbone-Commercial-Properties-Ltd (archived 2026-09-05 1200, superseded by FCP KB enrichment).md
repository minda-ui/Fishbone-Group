## Summary
The group's commercial property company, a separate legal entity from Fishbone Properties Ltd and, like it, under common control with Fishbone Holdings Ltd rather than a subsidiary. It owns the freehold of 145 High Street East, Wallsend, whose retail unit is let to Food Land, and is carrying out structural works on the building. Its only external borrowing is a loanback from the group's SSAS pension scheme (its FY2025 accounts show "secured loans" of £41,500, exactly the loanback's original principal), but it owed Construction £97,319 and Properties Ltd £103,251 at the last dates recorded. Its own FY2025 statutory accounts — 18 months to 30 April 2025 — have now been read from the Finance archive: a small trading company living on £18,000 of rent, £9,148 profit for the period and net assets of £10,562, its £240,559 building funded almost entirely by intercompany and pension borrowing. A dedicated knowledge base and Smartsheet workspace were started for it on 3 Sep 2026.

## Key facts
- Company number 13687238, incorporated in England and Wales, registered office in Wallsend. Directors M Gaudiesius and A Prutkovas. Accounting reference date moved from 31 October to 30 April; the 2025 accounts cover 18 months and were approved 30 April 2026. [S1]
- Under common control with Fishbone Holdings Ltd, Construction and Properties Ltd; described in Construction's accounts as "Fishbone Commercial Property Ltd, a company under common control". Not a subsidiary of Holdings. [S6 note 9][S7 note 10]
- Confirmed on 21 Aug 2026 as a genuinely separate company from Fishbone Properties Ltd, checked explicitly rather than assumed. [S2][S3]
- Asset: freehold of the two-storey building at 145 High Street East, Wallsend NE28 7RL. Ground floor retail unit of about 102 sq m let to Food Land at £1,000 per month plus VAT. Upper floor flats 2 and 2A Ferndale Avenue are a leasehold held by Fishbone Properties Ltd (their register FP 2202). Book cost £240,559. RICS valuation 5 Mar 2025 of the ground-floor interest £150,000, on the assumption that structural repairs are complete; they are not, estimated completion 1 Nov 2026. [S1][S8]
- Borrower on the SSAS Loanback (reference K0555): £41,500 original principal, 5.5% tracking base rate, 60-month term, balance £31,495.87 and £790.13 per month as at 21 Aug 2026, final payment due 28 Apr 2030. [S4] Its FY2025 accounts carry this loanback as "secured loans" of exactly £41,500 at 30 Apr 2025, split £7,436 due within one year and £34,064 due after — confirming the loanback is the only secured/external borrowing on the balance sheet. [S13]
- Net assets £10,562 at 30 Apr 2025 (share capital £100 plus £10,462 of reserves). The £213,226 of "other creditors" that looked undocumented in the earlier snapshot is now largely explained: it is dominated by the interest-free intercompany balances owed to Construction (£97,319) and Properties Ltd (£103,251), the accounts giving no related-party breakdown of their own. [S13][S6 note 9][S7 note 10]
- Strategic target: roughly one commercial acquisition every one to two years. Owner pay target referenced at £2k per month; £0 reported at the 18 Aug 2026 check-in. Managed by the Fishbone Properties team. [S5]

## Financial summary
Its first full statutory accounts read here: an 18-month period ended 30 April 2025 (the accounting reference date having moved from 31 October to 30 April), prepared under FRS 105 (the micro-entity regime), approved by the directors on 30 April 2026. - Turnover is the Food Land rent (£1,000/month, roughly 18 months). Administrative expenses break down as insurance £1,952, legal and professional £2,352, accountancy £2,800, subscriptions £417 and loan arrangement fees £395. Two employees (the directors). [S13]
- **Balance sheet at 30 Apr 2025.** Investment property £240,559, carried at cost (all additions in the period, no revaluation — the RICS £150,000 figure is not booked). Debtors £50; cash £19,775. Creditors due within one year £215,758 (corporation tax £2,146, other tax and social security £386, and other creditors £213,226 which itself includes the £7,436 current portion of the SSAS secured loan). Net current liabilities £195,933. Creditors due after one year £34,064 (the non-current SSAS secured loan). **Net assets £10,562.** [S13]
- The corporation tax return (CT600) covers the tax accounting period 1 Nov 2023 to 31 Oct 2024; box order is scrambled in the scanned copy. [S14]
- **Going concern.** The accounts are prepared on a going-concern basis. The company is balance-sheet solvent only by a thin margin (£10,562) and has large net current liabilities, but those current creditors are overwhelmingly the interest-free, no-fixed-term intercompany balances owed to Construction and Properties Ltd, i.e. group funding rather than third-party demand debt; the directors sign off on that basis. [S13]
