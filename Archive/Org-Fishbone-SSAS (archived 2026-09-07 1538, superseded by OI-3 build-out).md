## Summary
The group's Small Self-Administered Scheme (SSAS) pension fund. It is intended to act as an internal financing vehicle for group lending where appropriate, and already has one loanback to Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd. On Drive its paperwork sits under "Staff (SSAS)" in the Collaboration Space rather than in a folder of its own. This is a stub created on 2026-09-03 from existing Drive material.

## Key facts
- Intended role: internal financing or "bank" vehicle for group lending. [S1]
- Lender on the SSAS Loanback (reference K0555) to Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd: £41,500 principal, 5.5% tracking base rate, 60 months, £790.13 per month, balance £31,495.87 as at 21 Aug 2026, final payment due 28 Apr 2030. [S2]
- The Loans Wiki flags that compliance with SSAS and HMRC loanback rules (security, interest rate, term limits) has not been verified. [S2]
- Scheme documents on file: trust deed, board minutes, a credit score report, member documents for Minda and Andrej, a redeclaration folder, a Metro Bank account folder, and a 2023 folder. [S3]
- Member folders exist for Minda, Irina and Andrejus. [S3]
- The scheme's bank account appears to be with Metro Bank. [S3]
