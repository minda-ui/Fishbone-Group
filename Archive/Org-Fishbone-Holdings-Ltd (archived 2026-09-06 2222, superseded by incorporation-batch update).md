## Summary
The legal parent of three group companies (Fishbone Construction Ltd, Fishbone Waste Ltd and Furniture by Fishbone Ltd) and a lender to a fourth (Fishbone Properties Ltd), which it does not own but which is under common control. A pure holding company with no trade, two directors, two £1 shares, and net assets of £436,959 at 30 April 2025. Its only income is interest on the Properties Ltd loans and, in FY2025, a £128,000 dividend from Construction. Minda sees it as the natural home for group-level strategy, governance and capital allocation.

### Loans to Fishbone Properties Ltd

[S1 note 8][S4 note 7]

### Relationship to the rest of the group
The group is a legal group of four (Holdings plus three subsidiaries) beside two sister companies under common control (Properties Ltd and Commercial Properties Ltd) and the SSAS. Holdings' balance sheet is almost entirely the Properties Ltd loans and small subsidiary balances. Its FY2025 profit came from the £128,000 dividend Construction paid up in a year when Construction itself made a loss, which is worth noting for any group cash-flow view.
