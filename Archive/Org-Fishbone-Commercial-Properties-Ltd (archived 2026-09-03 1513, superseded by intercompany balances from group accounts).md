## Summary
The group's commercial property company, a separate legal entity from Fishbone Properties Ltd and managed by the same team. Its only recorded borrowing is a loanback from the group's SSAS pension scheme. A dedicated knowledge base was started for it on 3 Sep 2026. This is a stub created on 2026-09-03 from existing Drive material.

## Key facts
- Confirmed on 21 Aug 2026 as a genuinely separate company from Fishbone Properties Ltd, checked explicitly rather than assumed. [S1][S2]
- Borrower on the SSAS Loanback (reference K0555): £41,500 original principal, 5.5% tracking base rate, 60-month term, balance £31,495.87 and £790.13 per month as at 21 Aug 2026, final payment due 28 Apr 2030. [S3]
- This is the only facility recorded against the company; the Loans Wiki flags that a single-facility picture may be incomplete. [S1]
- Strategic target: roughly one commercial acquisition every one to two years. Owner pay target referenced at £2k per month; £0 reported at the 18 Aug 2026 check-in. [S4]
- Managed by the Fishbone Properties team. [S4]
