# Owner note — AT UK Interiors Ltd is out of scope

**Date:** 2026-09-05
**From:** Minda (owner), in session
**Type:** owner-note

## Statement (verbatim intent)

AT UK Interiors Ltd has no connection to the Fishbone Group. It is not a group company and is not under common control with the group.

## Context / commentary (assistant)

AT UK Interiors Ltd had appeared only in Fishbone Construction Ltd's HSBC bank statements (SRC-08, the Construction Knowledge Base), as a counterparty to a £15,000 credit labelled as a loan on 22 July 2026. On 2026-09-05T12:00Z it was flagged for a scope decision alongside Anthill Homes Ltd. This note resolves that: AT UK Interiors Ltd is **not** part of, or connected to, the group. The bank line is therefore an ordinary third-party transaction, not an intercompany balance, and does not make AT UK a group entity. Do not raise AT UK Interiors as a group company again.

This mirrors the earlier Anthill Homes Ltd ruling (owner note 2026-09-03, OI-2). The group remains final at seven entities (Construction, Properties, Commercial Properties, Holdings, Waste, Amfa Furniture, SSAS).
