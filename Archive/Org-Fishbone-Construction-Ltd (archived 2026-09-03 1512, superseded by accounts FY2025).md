## Summary
The construction arm of the Fishbone Group, serving the group's own property companies and external clients. Formerly named Fishbone Drylining Ltd; the two names are the same legal entity, not two companies. It carries the majority of the group's borrowing. This is a stub created on 2026-09-03 from existing Drive material; the underlying documents have not yet been processed through Raw.

## Key facts
- Formerly known as Fishbone Drylining Ltd. Same legal entity, confirmed by Minda on 21 Aug 2026. Several loan facilities were drawn under the former name. [S1]
- One of three group borrowing entities tracked in the Loans Wiki, alongside Fishbone Properties Ltd and Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd. [S1][S2]
- Carried roughly £517,196 of loan balance and about £23,706 per month of debt service across 11 of the group's 14 facilities as at 21 Aug 2026. [S1]
- Facilities recorded: two director loans re-lent by Minda personally (NatWest, HSBC), a related-party loan (Eugene), LendingCrowd, four Funding Circle fixed-term loans, Nucleus ENABLE, an HSBC business loan, and a Haydock Finance hire-purchase agreement on three woodworking machines. [S1]
- Also draws on two revolving Funding Circle FlexiPay trade-finance books (Book 1 and Book 2). [S1][S2]
- Operational trades historically covered: drylining, joinery, decorating, glazing partitions, floor sanding, painting, tiling, plumbing, electrics, fencing, roofing, gutters, mastic, drainage, LVT and carpet installation. [S3]
- Owner pay target referenced at £2k to £3k per month; £3,750 per month reported at the 18 Aug 2026 check-in. [S3]
- Primary email domain: fishboneconstruction.co.uk. Drive folders are owned by info@ and minda@ at that domain. [S4]
