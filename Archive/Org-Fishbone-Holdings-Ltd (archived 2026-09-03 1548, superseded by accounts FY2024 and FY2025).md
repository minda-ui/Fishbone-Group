# Fishbone Holdings Ltd

**Type:** Organisation
**Status:** Active
**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-03
**Related:** [Fishbone Properties Ltd](Org-Fishbone-Properties-Ltd.md) · [Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd](Org-Fishbone-Commercial-Properties-Ltd.md) · [Fishbone Construction Ltd](Org-Fishbone-Construction-Ltd.md)

## Summary
A group company that acts as an internal lender: it holds two intercompany loans to Fishbone Properties Ltd and appears in intercompany balances with Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd. Minda sees it as the natural home for group-level strategy, governance and capital allocation. It has no Drive folder, no Smartsheet workspace and no Loans Wiki page. Whether it is the legal parent of the other companies is not recorded anywhere surveyed. This is a stub created on 2026-09-03.

## Key facts
- Confirmed by Minda on 2026-09-03 as a member of the Fishbone Group, added after the initial list of six. [S1]
- Described in the Minda Wiki as an "additional group company" and, on 18 Aug 2026, as the natural candidate for group-level strategy, governance and capital allocation. [S2]
- Lender on two intercompany loans to Fishbone Properties Ltd: £283,000 at 3.80% and £20,000 at 6.00%, combined interest about £996.17 per month. On 31 Aug 2026 Fishbone Properties Ltd drafted a formal letter (document FP0000001, status Draft) asking the Holdings board to waive further interest and to minute the decision on both sides. [S3]
- Fishbone Properties Ltd's accounts for the year to 30 Apr 2024 record an intercompany balance with Holdings of £310,340. Direction not stated in the register summary; consistent with Holdings as lender. [S3]
- Fishbone Commercial Properties Ltd's knowledge base lists "intercompany balances with Drylining, Properties Ltd and Holdings" as something its proposed monthly financial snapshot would track, so Holdings also has dealings with that company. [S4]

### Relationship to the rest of the group
Holdings sits on the lending side of the group: Properties Ltd owes it about £303,000 of principal, and the group is asking it to stop charging interest. Whether it also holds the shares of the operating companies, and therefore whether "Fishbone Group" is a legal group or a family of commonly owned companies, is an open question that changes how consolidated figures should be presented.
