## Summary
A waste removal and disposal business, wholly owned by Fishbone Holdings Ltd. It was originally named **Rubbish Taxi NE Ltd** — the name under which it filed its corporation tax return for the year to 30 April 2023 — and had been renamed Fishbone Waste Ltd by the time of the YE 30/04/2024 accounts. [S11][S1] It has never made a profit: losses of £50,078 in FY2023, £46,054 in FY2024 and £90,435 in FY2025 left it with net liabilities of £197,152 at 30 April 2025, funded almost entirely by loans from Fishbone Construction Ltd and Fishbone Holdings Ltd. Despite that, the FY2025 accounts (approved 28 April 2026) show it **still trading** on a going-concern basis, with six employees, turnover of £111,882 and £51,384 of new assets bought in the year, including a £22,499 vehicle and £25,000 of leasehold improvements. The company **ceased operating in January 2026** and is deliberately **not being liquidated**: it keeps all the waste permits and certificates needed for waste removal so that it can support Fishbone Construction and Fishbone Properties projects. That reconciles the FY2025 accounts (trading to April 2025), the Minda Wiki's "inactive" (Aug 2026) and Companies House still showing it Active. [S10] Minda regards it as an important institutional lesson about numbers, accountability and operating discipline, to be preserved rather than treated only as a failed company.

### Reading the numbers
The business roughly doubled turnover in its second full year (FY2024) but administrative costs doubled too, mostly wages and vehicle running. FY2025 turnover held near £112,000 while admin costs rose again to £175,749, deepening the loss. It was financed by Construction and Holdings lending it money rather than by external debt; group undertakings owed rose to £212,709. Construction's FY2025 accounts still carry the £128,109 as a debtor, so if Waste is wound up without repaying, that is a write-off in Construction's books.
