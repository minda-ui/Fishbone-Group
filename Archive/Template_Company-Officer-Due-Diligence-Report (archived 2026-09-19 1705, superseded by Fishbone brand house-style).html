Filling this report

Confidence-tier every fact: Verified = read from the live Companies House register or a filed CH document; Provisional = an unverified lead (web search etc.) pending a register check; Gap = not established. Never present a Provisional item as fact.

Data to pull (via a Hub request assigned to Peter, whose Companies House routine reaches the live API): /search/officers?q=NAME → the subject's officer ID(s); /officers/{id}/appointments → every appointment per ID (company, role, appointed/resigned, particulars); and per company /company/{n}, /officers, /persons-with-significant-control, /charges, /filing-history. Disambiguate namesakes by month/year of birth + address.
Data boundary (strict for third parties): public register data only. For an individual, only public officer particulars — month/year of birth, nationality, country of residence, service/correspondence address, and occupation if published. Never a full date of birth, a residential/home address, or any non-public personal, banking, payroll or health data. Purpose limited to legitimate business due diligence.
