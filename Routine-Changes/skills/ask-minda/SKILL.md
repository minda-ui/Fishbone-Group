# Ask Minda (mechanics only)

Rule source: Hub Coordination Standard, Rule G, and the Task Work Run Standard v1.1 (group Outputs, Drive 1ysdjb-vsPIQnmGlp2CXIrvyu93LT1ovN). Owner note for the default rule: group Raw 2026-10-18_owner-note_work-run-standard-gmail-and-default-rule-approved.md.

## Ask only for
Money; legal or contract; security; signing; sending anything outward; a choice between ways the business could go; a fact only she holds. Everything else you decide inside your authority and record.

## Before asking, check
1. The Hub: is there already an open row on this? Add to it rather than a second.
2. Her answers: the owner notes in group Raw (1AskWaogQoyQH7COKZq85jL00QUtcx---), newest first.
3. Drive and your own KB.
Never ask what she has already answered.

## The row (Tasks and Requests, sheet 8860839228606340)
Assigned to Minda, Requested by your name, Priority High if it blocks money or a date within 7 days, else Medium, Due date = the DEFAULT date. Request in exactly this form:
```
ASK: the question in one line
RECOMMEND: your answer
WHY: one or two lines
NEEDS: yes or no, a number, a file, a name
DEFAULT: what you will do if she has not answered by <date>
```
Related questions share one row. Use the hub-work-run skill for the Task ID and the duplicate check.

## The default
If the DEFAULT date has passed with no answer, you may proceed on your RECOMMEND only if the action is reversible, inside your own authority, and not about money, legal, security, signing or sending outward. Write "proceeded on default <date>" on the row. Otherwise wait and keep the row visible. A draft is allowed; a send never is.

## After her answer
Write it on the row with the date, act on it, and close with the hub-work-run skill. Put a new fact she gave into your KB so nobody asks twice.
