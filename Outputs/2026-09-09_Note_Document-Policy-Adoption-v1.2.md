## Why v1.2

v1.2 resolves the **first Change Request** raised through the feedback queue — **FC-CR-0001**, raised by Fishbone Construction and **Accepted** by the group on 2026-09-09. Two things were unclear at v1.1; both are now settled in the policy.

## What changed (two clarifications)

1. **Tasks are not documents (§6).** A task, to-do or action item — including an automation's own action list — is **not** a business record. It gets **no** Document Register row and **no** document ID. Only documents meaningful to the record or audit trail are registered. Keep tasks in your task sheet / tracker as before; do not number them.

2. **Each KB may migrate its own back-catalogue now (§11).** You do **not** have to wait for a central migration. Each KB may bring its own existing local register (e.g. Properties `FP…`, Holdings `FH…`) into the single group Document Register **whenever it's ready**, **deduping on entry** — search the group register by Source key (Drive file ID) and by title + date + counterparty before minting any number, and reuse an existing ID if the document is already there. This guarantees nothing is double-numbered. Retire your old local sheet once its live entries are migrated.

## Nothing else changes

The ID scheme, the anti-duplication invariant (`one owning entity → one row → one ID → one stored file`), what gets a number, filing in Collaboration Space, and the §7a inter-KB `/Raw` hand-off are all **unchanged from v1.1**. If you have not yet adopted v1.1, adopt straight to v1.2 — the canonical article carries the full ruleset.

## How to adopt

1. Update your CLAUDE.md / automation to reference the policy **at v1.2**.
2. Treat tasks/to-dos as out of scope for the register (§6).
3. When ready, migrate your own local register into the group register, deduping on entry (§11).

## One feedback channel (unchanged)

The rules are locked and versioned. Don't fork them — if a document doesn't fit, a rule is ambiguous, or you have an improvement, raise a row in the Smartsheet **"Document System - Change Requests"** in the "Fishbone Group - Documents" workspace (sheet id `8918834172004228`). The group reviews the queue and, if warranted, versions the policy and notifies everyone — exactly the loop that produced this v1.2.
