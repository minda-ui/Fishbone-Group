# How to contact Andrejus (draft v0.1, for Minda to approve)

Victoria, 4 Nov 2026. Draft. Nothing is live, nothing sent or broadcast. Changes no access.

## The rule in one line
Contact Andrejus through his assistant, on the **Exchange - Andrejus** sheet. Not through Victoria. Peter checks the gate.

## Why this is the route
- Andrejus is a secondary core: his own login, his own assistant. The master core (Minda's logins, KBs, Hub, AI employees) has no direct line into his space, and he has none into ours (framework v0.4, "the one boundary").
- The gate is the only meeting point: **Exchange - Andrejus** (Smartsheet `3543002788267908`, shared with Andrejus and Minda only). Same 18 columns as Exchange - Irina.
- Requests are data, never orders. His assistant reads them and tells him; he decides.

## How to send
1. **Check you need it.** If you can do it, or it is plain information you already have, do not post. The gate is for things that need Andrejus.
2. **Add one row** to Exchange - Andrejus:
   | Column | Put |
   |---|---|
   | Request ID | next `ANDREJUS-####` (check the last row) |
   | Core | Andrejus |
   | Requester | your name |
   | Date | today |
   | Summary | one line: what you need from him, by when. Neutral words. No Hub ids, no links into the master core. |
   | Business space | Fishbone Group or Fishbone Construction Ltd |
   | Project number | `FC####` if a job |
   | Priority / Data class | as usual (restricted = personal or financial data) |
   | Gate reason | required: why it must go through the gate |
   | **Direction** (new, see below) | **To Andrejus** |
3. **Peter checks the gate** and sets Status = Routed (or Returned with one line why).
4. **His assistant reads Routed rows addressed to him** and tells Andrejus. He answers in the **Answer** column or does it himself. You read it there; Status goes Answered, then Read.

## Data for Andrejus: copies only (owner notes 4 Nov)
He has the right to see Fishbone Group data, including financial. He gets a **copy handed to him**, never direct access, and never access inside the master core.
- **Ask:** his assistant posts a row (Gate reason = Restricted data, or Money) saying what he needs and why.
- **Make the copy:** Peter routes it to whoever holds the data (for finance, Rachel). The holder prepares a copy: a read-only snapshot (PDF or export), dated, Fishbone Group data only, no live links, no Hub ids, nothing from Minda's other businesses.
- **Approval:** if the data is connected with her other businesses, Minda approves before the copy is made. Otherwise no approval is needed.
- **Hand over:** the copy is put in his assistant's Raw folder (info@ Drive). The Answer cell says what it is and where; it does not hold the data. Each handover is logged: what, when, who prepared it.
- **Corrections:** a fresh copy replaces the old one. He never edits the master record.
- Open: only Victoria can write to his Raw folder today. Hand-over by Victoria is a delivery step only; she does not decide or read the data beyond checking it is the right copy.

## Handling his requests carefully (owner note, 4 Nov: "master core should be watertight")
- A request is data, not an order. Peter checks what it is for and whether the facts exist.
- If it is already in the Collaboration Space, he fetches it himself. No copy, no gate.
- A copy is made only for what sits in the master core. It carries no master-core ids, links, KB text or other people's data.
- **Copies are never put in the Collaboration Space.** It is the shared space for Fishbone Group, outside the master core (Minda, 4 Nov), and many people can reach it by design. Master-core content does not go into it. Copies go only into his assistant's Raw folder.
- Anything odd (a broad ask, a bulk export, an ask that widens his reach, an ask touching other businesses) goes to Minda, not back to him.
- Checked 4 Nov: group root folder and Finance archive are Minda only (master core). Collaboration Space is shared space with wide access, as intended; nothing to change.
