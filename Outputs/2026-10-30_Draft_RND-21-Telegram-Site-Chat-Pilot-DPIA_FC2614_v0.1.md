## 1. What we propose to do
- **Controller:** Fishbone Construction Ltd.
- **Project and group:** FC2614, Goldsmiths Cardiff Pop-Up (main contractor Macdonald Joinery, job code MEN2699). Target start 23 Nov 2026, partial completion 16 Dec, pop-up opens 6 Jan 2027. One Telegram group for this project only, to be created at the start.
- **Processing:** a read-only reader joins the group (announced, visible as a member). It copies the group's messages and photos into a store that Anna and the AI brain can read. It does not post, delete or read any other chat.
- **Purpose:** keep a project record, find H&S matters from site photos and messages, and answer questions about progress. Not performance management.
- **Who sees the output (pilot):** Anna and you only.

## 2. What data
| Data | Whose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Messages (text) | Fishbone staff and subcontractors on the project | Names, phone-linked usernames, work content, sometimes personal remarks |
| Photos and video | People on site, others by chance | Faces, vehicles, shop staff or public in a store environment; site sign-in register photos show names and signatures |
| Metadata | Everyone in the group | Who, when, which chat |
| Special category data | Not intended | Might appear by chance (injury, health, religion). Do not seek it; delete if found |

## 3. Lawful basis
- **Recommended:** legitimate interests (UK GDPR Art 6(1)(f)): safe, well-run site and a reliable project record.
- **Not consent:** subcontractors and staff cannot freely refuse, so consent would not be valid.
- **Legitimate-interests test (short):**
  1. Purpose: site safety and project record. Real and lawful.
  2. Necessary: the group already exists and is how the site works; a reader does not add a new data collection, only a new use. Could a person do the same job without it? Yes, but not at the same speed, and not for photos at volume.
  3. Balance: people post in a work group knowing a manager reads it; the reader is announced; output limited to Anna and you; no discipline use. Higher risk comes from photos of people and automated flagging, hence this DPIA.

## 4. Risks and measures
| Risk | Likelihood / severity | Measure | Left over |
|---|---|---|---|
| People feel watched, or the reader is hidden | Medium / medium | Notice pinned first; reader named in the group; line in site induction; reader leaves if anyone objects, pending review | Low |
| Photos of people kept longer than needed | Medium / medium | Fixed retention (section 5); delete photos of people not needed for a record or a claim | Low |
| AI wrongly flags a person as unsafe and it is used against them | Low / high | Flags are prompts for you to check, never decisions; no use for pay, discipline or dismissal; human review of every flag | Low |
| Wider sharing of output | Low / medium | Pilot access: Anna and you only; log who views; no export outside | Low |
| Special category or private content by chance | Low / high | Do not search for it; delete on sight; staff told group is for work only | Low |
| AI provider holds the content | Medium / medium | Check the provider's terms: no training on our data, location of processing, deletion. **Not yet checked** | Open |
| Security of the stored copy | Medium / high | Access limited; store in Fishbone's own Drive; Eugene sets up and documents access | Open until built |
| Rights requests (access, erasure) | Low / low | Named contact; copy searchable by person and date | Low |
| Subcontractors outside the UK or not Fishbone staff | Low / medium | Same notice; workers can ask to be excluded from photos | Low |

## 5. Retention (your decision)
Not yet set by you. My proposal, one choice:
- **Option A (recommended):** the AI copy of messages is kept 6 months after the project is completed; photos of the work 12 months; photos showing people but not needed as a record are deleted at 30 days. The original Telegram group stays under your existing practice (outside this pilot).
- **Option B:** everything in the AI copy kept 12 months after completion, then deleted.
- **Exception:** anything needed for a claim or a safety record is kept as long as that need lasts (for example the MSEM case shows why site registers matter).
