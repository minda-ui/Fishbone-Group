# Privacy notice: staff and subcontractors

**Fishbone Construction Ltd** (company number 07948220), 6 Beverley Place, Wallsend, NE28 7BH ("Fishbone", "we"), is the controller of your personal data. Contact: Mindaugas Gaudiesius, Managing Director, minda@fishboneconstruction.co.uk, +44 7855 463292, or info@fishboneconstruction.co.uk.

This notice is for people who work for us as employees or as self-employed subcontractors, and for people who apply to. It does not cover our customers or our property business.

## What we hold
- **Everyone:** name, contact details, date of birth where needed, photographs and videos from site, messages you send to us about work, the days and sites you worked.
- **Employees:** National Insurance number, right-to-work documents, bank details, pay and hours, tax code, pension details, payslips, any absence or accident records, training and site cards (for example CSCS) [CONFIRM which].
- **Subcontractors:** your company or trading name, UTR and CIS verification, bank details, invoices or timesheets, insurance details, site cards [CONFIRM which].
- **Health and safety:** accident or near-miss records, site induction and sign-in registers.
- We do not collect health, ethnicity or religion information unless you give it to us for a reason (for example an accident report or a reasonable adjustment). We keep that information to the minimum needed.

## Why we use it, and our legal basis
| Purpose | Basis |
|---|---|
| Paying you, running payroll, pension, tax and CIS returns | Contract; legal obligation |
| Checking your right to work in the UK | Legal obligation |
| Running sites safely, site sign-in, accident records | Legal obligation; legitimate interests |
| Planning work, recording progress, project records and photos | Legitimate interests |
| Dealing with disputes and claims (for example a contract dispute) | Legitimate interests; legal claims |
| Answering your questions and keeping in touch | Legitimate interests |

## Who we share it with
- **HMRC** and, where the law requires, other public bodies.
- **Our accountants** [CONFIRM: AGGA Services Ltd and RMT Accountants, as applicable] and our payroll and bookkeeping providers [CONFIRM: QuickBooks (Intuit) and any payroll provider].
- **Main contractors and clients** where a site needs your name or details for access, security clearance or sign-in (for example the BBC site passes).
- **Insurers and advisers** (for example solicitors) where needed for a claim or advice.
- **Software we use** to store and process the information: Google Workspace, QuickBooks, Smartsheet, Telegram, WhatsApp [CONFIRM list].
- **AI assistants.** We use AI assistants to help with administration, for example drafting emails and checking records. They may read documents that contain your details. They work for us under our instructions; they do not make decisions about your pay or work [CONFIRM wording and the provider terms].

We do not sell your data. We do not send it outside the UK except through the services above [CONFIRM where those services store data].

## How long we keep it
- Payroll, tax and CIS records: as long as the law requires, generally up to six years [CONFIRM with accountant].
- Right-to-work documents: the time you work for us and a period after [CONFIRM with an adviser].
- Site, health and safety and accident records: as the law and our insurers require.
- Project records, photos and messages: as set in each project's notice (for the FC2614 pilot, 12 months after the project is completed).
- Anything needed for a claim is kept until the claim and the time to bring it have ended.

## Your rights
You can ask us to show you your data, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or move it, and you can object to our use under legitimate interests. Contact us using the details above. We will answer within one month. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.

## Changes
We will tell you if we change this notice in a way that matters. Last updated: [date].
