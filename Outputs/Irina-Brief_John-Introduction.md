# Meet John — a new AI assistant for the property business

*A short brief to introduce John to Irina (property manager, Fishbone Properties Ltd). Prepared 2026-09-19.*

---

Hi Irina,

We've set up an AI assistant called **John** to help with the day-to-day of Fishbone Properties. Think of him
as a fast, tireless admin helper working behind the scenes — he takes the paperwork load off you so you can
focus on the properties, the tenants and the decisions.

**What John does (the admin):**
- Sorts the property inbox and files what comes in.
- Numbers and files documents; keeps the property register and task list up to date; keeps the records
  straight.
- **Prepares drafts for you** — a tenant letter, a notice, a compliance to-do — ready for you to check.

**What John never does (the important bit):**
- He **never sends anything to a tenant or anyone outside**, never signs anything, never commits the company,
  and never touches money or official filings.
- Everything that goes *out* goes out through **you**. John prepares it; **you review and release it.**
  Nothing reaches a tenant without your say-so.

**So the one change for you:**
- When there's something to go out, **John emails you the finished draft** marked *"draft for your review /
  release."* You check it and decide whether it goes — and you're the one who sends it or serves it.
- He doesn't replace you or decide for you — he does the legwork; your judgement and sign-off stay exactly
  where they are.

**Your information stays safe:** tenant details stay inside the secure, access-controlled system throughout.
John keeps them in, never shares them out.

Any questions, or if it doesn't feel right at any point, just tell Minda and we'll adjust.
