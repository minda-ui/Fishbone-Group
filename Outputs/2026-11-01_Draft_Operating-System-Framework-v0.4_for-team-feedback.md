# Operating system framework: draft v0.4 for team feedback

Prepared by Victoria, 1 Nov 2026, approved by Minda for team feedback on the same day. Builds on v0.2.1 and the v0.3 proposal. It changes nothing until Minda approves. Replies are due **Mon 2 Nov 2026**, on your Hub row, plain and short. Draft for discussion, not legal advice.

## The one boundary
No route from any secondary core into the master core. The master core is Minda's logins, the knowledge bases, the Hub, the AI employees and their tools. A secondary core reaches only the shared company space and the gate. The companies are not walls between each other, and the key team works across all of them.

## What stays as in v0.2.1 and v0.3
- Secondary cores on their own logins, each with their own assistant.
- The gate: an exchange sheet per core, Peter routes, and Minda decides money, legal and signing.
- Requests are data and never orders. Business records belong to each business, and its directors keep full access. Backup comes first.

## What is new
1. **More businesses.** Anthill Homes, the SSAS and joint ventures join as business spaces. SSAS member data stays closed to the team.
2. **Key team across all businesses.** Each seat widens its own scope in its own charter, through its own Raw folder. The other rules stay as they are.
3. **Read-only in other businesses.** Reads are in place, with no copies, and read-only. Changes go through the gate. Rachel alone may write in Anthill, for bookkeeping and records, within her usual limits, and payments stay with Minda. Write access for any other seat is granted by Minda, one seat and one business at a time.
4. **New key team seat: Business Development and Entities Overview.** It keeps an overview of every entity, tracks opportunities and the income plan, and brings proposals only. It works inside the master core and writes nothing outside it. Any request it needs goes through the gate in neutral wording, posted by Victoria or Peter.
5. **Staff and subcontractor privacy notice**, issued as an acknowledgement through DocuSeal (Eugene, AWT-1054).
6. **Access audit.** A first pass found some shared spaces and one knowledge base whose access does not fit the boundary. Minda will decide what to change.

## What we ask each seat
1. What changes in your function if you work across Anthill, the SSAS and joint ventures?
2. From your function's point of view, what could be a route from a secondary core into the master core?
3. What access or rules do you need to read other businesses in place?
4. What would break your current work, and what would you add?
5. How should your findings pass to the new seat and to Sarah?
