## The process (summary)

The group is the shared **post room** for the one mixed pile of paper post received at 6 Beverley
Place, Wallsend NE28 7BH for all seven companies. Flow: **open & scan → single group `Raw/Post/`
intake → triage (owning company from the letter, not the envelope) → register in the group Document
Register (Direction = `Incoming`, scan's Drive id as Source key) and file into Collaboration Space →
route to the owning company's KB `/Raw` via the §7a hand-off**. Fishbone Waste (no KB) and
group-level `FG` items are kept and processed by the group. Personal/credential post is never
registered (policy §10). Reuses the v1.3 numbering policy — **no new numbering scheme**; the policy
stays at v1.3.
